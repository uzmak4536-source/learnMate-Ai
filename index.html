📘 LearnMate AI
A student prototype supporting SDG 4: Quality Education
UN Sustainable Development Goal 4

Learning support, made
simple and accessible.

LearnMate is a demonstration of a personalized learning assistant. Choose a topic, get a clear explanation, and check your understanding with a short quiz. It is designed to support—not replace—teachers.

📚 Learn a Topic

Select a sample topic and generate a beginner-friendly explanation.

🧠 Quick Knowledge Check

Try a sample question and receive immediate feedback.

Which statement best describes an algorithm?

🌱 How this supports SDG 4

🎯
Self-paced learning
Students can review a topic at a pace that suits them.
💬
Simple explanations
Learning content is broken into clear, manageable ideas.
✅
Practice and feedback
A short quiz helps students check their understanding.
🌍
Inclusive access
The concept can be extended with regional-language support and low-bandwidth access.

💡 Future Improvements

  • Add more school and college subjects.
  • Introduce verified AI-generated explanations with teacher review.
  • Add regional-language translation.
  • Track quiz scores privately to recommend revision topics.
  • Improve accessibility for mobile devices and slower internet.

Prototype note: this demo uses sample, pre-written content and a sample quiz. It does not connect to an AI service or store student data.
